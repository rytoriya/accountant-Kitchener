Accounting Built Around
Your Goals

Accountant Kitchener provides trusted, end-to-end financial management for individuals, entrepreneurs, and growing businesses across Kitchener. We specialize in tax filing, bookkeeping, payroll, and CPA advisory services, delivered with the precision of a large firm and the personal attention of a local accountant.

Whether you are a student filing your first return, a self-employed professional managing quarterly HST, or a small business owner navigating corporate tax, we have the expertise to guide you through every step clearly and efficiently.

Our Mission

To be the most accessible, trusted, and results-focused accounting firm in Kitchener. Every client, regardless of the size of their business or complexity of their return, deserves clear advice, prompt service, and a partner they can rely on year after year.

Our Story

Accountant Kitchener was built after recognizing a common challenge: business owners and individuals were spending too much time dealing with financial complexity and too little time on growth. We built a practice that combines deep CPA expertise with genuine accessibility, where your calls get answered, your questions are explained clearly, and your deadlines are never missed.

Serving Kitchener since 2019, we have helped hundreds of residents and businesses get their finances structured, compliant, and optimized.

Meet Ibtahaj Hashmi, CPA, FCCA

Ibtahaj Hashmi is a Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA Canada) and Fellow of the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (FCCA, UK) with over 18 years of experience across accounting, financial reporting, governance, and regulated environments in Canada and internationally.

An alumnus of Oxford and the University of Salford, Ibtahaj brings a rare combination of global perspective and local Kitchener expertise. His career has spanned corporate financial reporting, cash flow analysis, integrated accounting systems, and governance frameworks.

His Approach

Ibtahaj's practice philosophy is simple: clients should spend less time worrying about taxes and more time building their future. He provides structured, transparent, and responsive advisory, ensuring every client understands their financial position and options at each stage.
