# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two equal audiences, often in the same household:

- **Individuals in Kitchener-Waterloo** — T1 filers, employees, retirees, landlords, RRSP/TFSA planners. They are stressed at tax time, unsure what deductions they're missing, and want a real person (not software) who will look after them year-round.
- **Small-business owners in Kitchener** — sole proprietors and incorporated businesses needing bookkeeping, payroll, HST/GST filing, T2 returns, and strategic advice on structure (incorporate or not, salary vs. dividend). They are time-poor and have been burned by accountants who only call once a year.

Both arrive via local search ("accountant Kitchener", "CPA Kitchener"). Their first question is: *can I trust these people?* Their second is: *will they actually respond when I need them?*

## Product Purpose

Accountant Kitchener is the lead-generation website for IH CPA, a Kitchener accounting practice led by Ibtahaj Hashmi, CPA, FCCA (18+ years experience). The site's one job is to convert local search traffic into booked consultations. Success = a visitor submitting the contact form or calling (647) 564-9666.

Services: personal tax filing, small business tax, corporate accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, HST/GST filing, tax consulting, CPA advisory.

## Brand Personality

Expert, Precise, Confident

Tone of voice: direct, jargon-free, reassuring. The site speaks as a senior professional — not stiff, not casual. It earns trust through specificity (real credentials, real address, real team) rather than through warmth-by-design. It never sounds like a template.

## Anti-references

- **Big-4 / corporate cold** — KPMG, Deloitte, PwC: impersonal, intimidating, faceless. This firm is local and accessible, not a tower in Toronto.
- **Generic AI template aesthetic** — cream/beige backgrounds, tracked small-caps eyebrows above every section, identical icon-card grids. The current site already trends here; future work should route away from it.
- **Mass-market DIY** — TurboTax, H&R Block: commodity feel, cheapness signalling. This is a professional CPA practice, not a filing widget.

## Design Principles

1. **Earn trust through specificity.** Real credentials (CPA, FCCA), real address (142 Dubrick Crescent), real team headshots, real reviews. Never rely on vague "trusted" copy alone.
2. **Confidence over decoration.** The brand's authority comes from precision and restraint — clear hierarchy, purposeful whitespace, no decorative filler. Every element either earns its place or gets cut.
3. **Local, not generic.** "Kitchener" is a competitive advantage. Surface local signals: neighbourhood references, regional industry knowledge, proximity. Not a national franchise.
4. **Friction-free path to contact.** Every section has a clear next action toward booking a consultation. CTA copy is specific ("Book a free 30-minute consultation") not vague ("Get started").
5. **Readable before beautiful.** Body contrast ≥ 4.5:1, max 68ch line length, no decorative typography that sacrifices legibility. A professional's clients include 60-year-old retirees on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Particular attention to:
- Body text contrast (≥ 4.5:1) — muted gray on near-white currently borderlines in places.
- Keyboard navigability for the contact form and hamburger nav.
- Reduced-motion support for any scroll animations added in future.
- Mobile-first responsive: many local-search users arrive on phone.
